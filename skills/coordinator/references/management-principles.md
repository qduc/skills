# Transferable management principles

These are systems principles for coordinating agent work, not a reason to
import human people-management practices. Existing workflow rules are not
repeated here; candidate behavior changes remain experiments until evaluated.

| Principle | Source | Meaning for agent coordination | Applied in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coordinator output is team output; intervene at highest leverage | Grove, *High Output Management* | Spend coordinator attention on bottlenecks, shared decisions, and integration rather than duplicating bounded work. | [Decompose](../SKILL.md#2-understand-then-decompose); [Integrate](../SKILL.md#5-accept-and-integrate) |
| Task-relevant maturity | Grove, *High Output Management* | Brief detail should match demonstrated capability on this kind of task; test this as an experiment, not a blanket rule. | [Assign](../SKILL.md#3-assign-bounded-work); [Task-relevant brief detail](experiments.md#task-relevant-brief-detail) |
| Paired speed and quality indicators | Grove, *High Output Management* | Evaluate flow gains with a quality measure so faster coordination does not mask defective integration. | [Paired coordination indicators](experiments.md#paired-coordination-indicators) |
| Small batches, cost of delay, visible queues | Reinertsen, *Principles of Product Development Flow* | Small independently useful increments, explicit priority by delay cost, and visible waiting work; batch size and WIP already have candidates. | [Milestones](large-work.md#plan-milestones-and-waves); [WIP cap](experiments.md#wip-cap); [Cost-of-delay ordering](experiments.md#cost-of-delay-ordering); [Constraint-aware dispatch](experiments.md#constraint-aware-dispatch) |
| Find and protect the constraint | Goldratt, *The Goal* | Identify the limiting review, quota, or serial gate; avoid feeding it an unmanageable queue. | [Constraint-aware dispatch](experiments.md#constraint-aware-dispatch) |
| Conceptual integrity and coordination cost | Brooks, *The Mythical Man-Month* | Preserve one design/contract owner; parallelism adds coordination cost and is worthwhile only when work is genuinely separable. | [Decompose](../SKILL.md#2-understand-then-decompose); [Own contracts](large-work.md#own-contracts-at-the-seams) |
| Align work with module interfaces | Skelton & Pais, *Team Topologies*; Conway's law | Cut assignments at stable module boundaries so communication follows real ownership seams. | [Decompose](../SKILL.md#2-understand-then-decompose); [Own contracts](large-work.md#own-contracts-at-the-seams) |
| Pre-mortem | Klein | Before a large dispatch, imagine failure and identify plausible causes before committing dependent work. | [Dispatch pre-mortem](experiments.md#dispatch-pre-mortem) |
| Record decision rationale | Duke, *Thinking in Bets* | Preserve the evidence and reasoning behind material assumptions when the decision is made. | [Record material assumptions](decision-policy.md#record-material-assumptions) |
| Judge decisions by process, not outcome | Duke, *Thinking in Bets* | Assess a past decision against the information available when it was made, not against its result. Not yet applied; it is a candidate behavior change. | [Outcome-independent decision review](experiments.md#outcome-independent-decision-review) |

## Does not transfer

- Motivation, morale, career growth, and performance coaching do not map to stateless workers.
- Politics and one-on-ones do not map to agent coordination.
