“State-of-the-art” agent architectures for HumanEval do not outperform simple baselines.
There is no significant accuracy difference between our warming strategy and the best-performing agent architecture.
Meanwhile, the escalation strategy strictly improves accuracy while costing less than half of LDB (GPT-3.5).
