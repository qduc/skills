Evaluating Sakana's AI Scientist: Bold Claims, Mixed Results, and a Promising Future?
The system's literature reviews produced poor novelty assessments, often misclassifying established concepts.
42% of experiments failed due to coding errors.
Generated manuscripts were poorly substantiated, with a median of five citations, most outdated.
Despite these flaws, the AI Scientist represents a leap forward in research automation.
