# Method provenance

Inspected on 2026-10-05. These are methodological inspirations, not evidence for
future research answers. The skill is an original, tool-neutral synthesis.

- [SkillMedev: research-synthesis SKILL.md](https://github.com/SkillMedev/skills/blob/main/skills/research-synthesis/SKILL.md).
  Inspected the rendered skill, especially capture, grouping, tensions, gaps,
  and weighting guidance. Adapted claim-level notes and organizing conclusions
  across sources. Did not adopt its 20+ source framing or prescribed report
  structure. Its public availability does not establish popularity or proven
  effectiveness.
- [Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 9: Summarizing study characteristics and preparing for synthesis](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-09).
  Inspected the introductory framework and sections 9.3.1–9.3.2. Adapted checking
  context and comparability before grouping evidence, and using a compact matrix
  when helpful. This chapter addresses intervention reviews; the skill generalizes
  those comparison principles without claiming to implement Cochrane review
  standards, statistical synthesis, or a systematic literature review.

The scope, host-rule compatibility, untrusted-source boundary, evidence-lineage
checks, compositional inputs/outputs, and stopping rules were designed for this
primitive's bounded research role. Follow governing host instructions and actual
task requirements when they require a different tool, citation format, or depth.
