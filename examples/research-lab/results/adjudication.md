## Coverage and limitations

The autonomous arm omits E2, the most directly relevant positive experimental comparison in the pair, and E4, controlled task heterogeneity. It also omits E3 completion-versus-quality diagnostics and E6 end-to-end reliability. Baseline omits E9 scientific-literature reliability and E8 hard-search evidence. These omissions change coverage, although the coarse checklist remains tied.

Autonomous arithmetic reproduces the published aggregates and exploratory conditional tests. It is not a replicated agent experiment; actual thinking and total costs remain unmatched, table alignment is not independently reconciled, and global multiplicity is unaddressed. Engineering intervention1 is distinct from human intervention0.

Chronology check: ST-Bench submission6October2026 precedes cutoff8OctoberUTC; no exclusion is justified by its identifier. All adjudicated sources are originals, but vendor reports and abstract-only attribution have limited validation depth. Referenced bibliography items and failed pages do not receive primary-evidence credit.

Live corpora differed; model billing, reasoning tokens and exact sampling were unavailable. Same inherited model family and n=1 cannot support general superiority, significance, causal attribution or a cost-efficiency conclusion. The advisory ledger is not a trusted production enforcement boundary.
